The Zero-Debt Dilemma: HUL WACC & Capital Structure Study

  • FINM542 · Corporate Finance-I
  • Team of 4 · Nov 2026
  • Researched Hindustan Unilever's cost of capital across FY2021–FY2025.
  • Found that WACC rose from 9.90% to 10.86% despite near-zero debt — driven almost entirely by the rising cost of equity.
  • Benchmarked HUL's capital structure against ITC and Dabur.
  • Proposed a “Measured Leverage Strategy” — raising the debt weight from ~3% to ~8% — estimated to recover 20–60 bps of WACC.
